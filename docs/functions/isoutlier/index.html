isoutlier

Find outliers in data.

Try itView source help →

Find outliers in data.

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(A)

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(A, method)

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(A, 'percentiles', [lo hi])

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(A, movmethod, window)

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(..., dim)

Syntax

TF = isoutlier(..., Name, Value)

Syntax

[TF, L, U, C] = isoutlier(...)

Works along dim (default: the first dimension whose size is not 1) of numeric vectors and matrices, or on the numeric variables of tables and timetables. Methods: 'median' (default; more than k = 3 scaled MAD from the median, scaled MAD = c*median(abs(A-median(A))) with c = -1/(sqrt(2)*erfcinv(3/2))), 'mean' (more than k = 3 standard deviations from the mean), 'quartiles' (outside [Q1-k*IQR, Q3+k*IQR], k = 1.5, quartiles as quantile computes them; center (Q1+Q3)/2), 'percentiles' (outside the given percentiles; center their midpoint), 'grubbs' (two-sided Grubbs test removing one outlier per iteration at significance alpha = 0.05) and 'gesd' (generalized extreme Studentized deviate test with at most MaxNumOutliers outliers, default the integer nearest to 10 percent of the elements, at least 1); for these two the thresholds are mean +- critical value * std of the points kept and the center is their mean. 'movmedian' and 'movmean' apply the median and mean rules with local statistics over a window: a scalar k covers sample points in [t-k/2, t+k/2) (k samples centered, one extra behind for even k), [kb kf] covers [t-kb, t+kf]; windows shrink at the ends. NaN is never an outlier and is ignored by the statistics. L, U and C are the lower threshold, upper threshold and center: one value per vector, or one per element for moving methods. Options: 'ThresholdFactor' (k; alpha for grubbs and gesd), 'SamplePoints' (sorted unique numeric, datetime or duration vector; a timetable uses its row times and needs a duration window), 'DataVariables' (names, indices or a logical mask), 'OutputFormat' ('logical' gives a logical matrix of the table size, 'tabular' a table) and 'MaxNumOutliers'.

Example

isoutlier([2 3 2 4 3 50 3 2])

Example

[TF, L, U, C] = isoutlier([1 2 3 4 100], 'quartiles')

Example

isoutlier([1 1.2 0.9 6 1.1 1 0.8], 'movmedian', 3)

Differences from MATLAB: no N-D arrays, function-handle DataVariables or multicolumn table variables. MATLAB does not publish every center and threshold rule, so C for 'quartiles', 'grubbs' and 'gesd' and the gesd default count follow the definitions above.
